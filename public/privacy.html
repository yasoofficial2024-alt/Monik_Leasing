Privacy Policy

This application is an internal business system of MONIK GROUP OF COMPANIES - Department of Chairman Office, developed for managing hire purchase quotations, calculations and related business information.

1. Information We Handle

  • Customer and quotation information entered into the system.
  • Vehicle and financing details.
  • Selling price, down payment and discount information.
  • Interest rates and facility periods.
  • Document charges and other applicable charges.
  • Installment and amortization calculations.

2. Use of Information

Information processed through this application is used solely for authorized business operations, quotation preparation, financial calculations, record management and internal decision-making.

3. Data Protection

Access to the application and its information is restricted to authorized users. Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their access credentials and business information.

4. Data Sharing

Information will not be disclosed to unauthorized third parties except where required for legitimate business purposes, legal obligations or appropriate authorization.

5. Accuracy of Information

Users are responsible for ensuring that information entered into the system is accurate and up to date.

6. System Security

MONIK GROUP OF COMPANIES takes reasonable measures to protect information processed through this application against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or misuse.

7. Contact

For privacy or data-related enquiries, please contact the Department of Chairman Office, MONIK GROUP OF COMPANIES.
